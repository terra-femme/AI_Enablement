3 ways your team is quietly misusing AI

Same tool, three expensive habits — and the one-line fix for each.

1

Pasting confidential data into public AI

Client data, records, and secrets leave your control and can't be pulled back.

Use only sanctioned tools — never paste what you couldn't email to a stranger.
2

Trusting answers without checking

Confident, made-up information reaches clients with your name on it.

AI drafts, humans approve — verify before you send.
3

Treating it like a live search engine

Outdated facts and invented sources slip into real work.

Confirm anything factual — it reasons, it doesn't "look it up."
Think of AI as a brilliant new intern: fast and helpful, but you'd never hand it client secrets or send its first draft to a customer unchecked.
