# Lesson 5 — Saving Your Work
**Time:** 5 min · **Core message:** *No GitHub needed — your work lives in Projects + your drive's version history.*

## Objective
The room knows where AI work-in-progress lives, where finished work goes, and how to find it again.

## Demo first (2 min)
1. Open a **Project** in the approved tool; show a couple of chats grouped under it. "This is your saved workspace for an ongoing effort."
2. In OneDrive/SharePoint/Google Drive, right-click a file → **Version History** → restore an old version live. The "oh!" moment.

## The 3 homes (rule of 3)
1. **Inside the AI — work in progress.** Use **Projects** to group related chats and attach reference docs; your chat history is saved and searchable. Give chats clear titles.
2. **Your team drive — finished work.** Save outputs to [OneDrive/SharePoint/Drive]. **Version History is your "undo button across time"** — no more `final_v2_FINAL.docx`.
3. **A shared prompt list — what works.** Keep the team's best prompts in one place to reuse.

## The anchor analogy
> Developers use complicated tools to "save progress." **You already have the same power** — it's the **Version History** button in the drive you already use.

## Talking points / script beats
- "Stop saving to your desktop. It's invisible to the team and not backed up."
- "Name your chats and files like you'd want to find them in 3 months."

## Common stumble (and the fix)
- "Where did my chat go?" → It's in history/Projects; show the search. Fear of "losing it" is why people hoard messy copies.

> ⚠️ Presenter: confirm the firm's **actual** Project settings + retention in the admin console before stating specifics (see `../presenter/presenter-knowledge-base.md`).

## So what / now what
**Now what:** start a Project for any multi-day task; save finished work to the team drive, not your desktop.
