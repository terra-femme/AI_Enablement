# AI 101 — Common Questions & Answers (cheat sheet)
### Keep this open during Q&A. Answers are ELI5 and short on purpose.

> Golden rule: a short, honest answer beats a long, shaky one. If you don't know, say so and follow up.

## About what it is
**Q: Is it connected to the internet / live?**
A: Not by default. It learned from data up to a cutoff date. Some tools *add* live web access as a feature — but assume "not live" unless you know it's turned on.

**Q: Does it actually think / is it conscious?**
A: No. It predicts likely helpful language really well. Useful, not alive.

**Q: Will it replace my job?**
A: It replaces *tasks*, not judgment. It does the boring 70% so you focus on the 30% only you can do. People who use it well are the ones who do well.

## About trust & accuracy
**Q: Can I trust what it says?**
A: Trust the *wording*, verify the *facts*. It can be confidently wrong. You're accountable for anything you send.

**Q: Why did it make up a source/quote?**
A: It generates plausible-sounding text; sometimes "plausible" isn't "real." Always check citations.

**Q: It gave two different answers to the same question — why?**
A: Normal. It's generating, not looking up one fixed record. For facts, verify; for wording, pick the best.

## About data & privacy (the big fear)
**Q: Does it train on what I type?**
A: In our **approved enterprise tool**, business data is handled under our enterprise agreement. ⚠️ *Presenter: confirm exact current terms in the admin console before answering definitively.* The real danger is **personal/free** AI accounts — don't use those for work.

**Q: Can I paste a client contract / patient info / spreadsheet?**
A: Only in the approved tool, and only at the data level it's approved for. Quick test: *if you couldn't email it to a stranger, don't paste it.*

**Q: Who can see my chats?**
A: Per company settings/retention. Treat anything you enter as reviewable by the company.

## About using it
**Q: I'm not technical — is it hard?**
A: No. If you can write an email, you can use it. Just brief it like a person (Context, Role, Example, Format).

**Q: What's the difference between the chat, the coding tool, and Cowork?**
A: Chat = everyday writing/thinking. The coding tool = for developers. Cowork = it does multi-step work for you. Most of you live in chat and Projects.

**Q: Which "model" should I pick?**
A: Default is fine for most work. Bigger/most-capable models for hard reasoning; faster ones for quick stuff. ⚠️ *Presenter: confirm the firm's current model options before naming them.*

## About safety/scams
**Q: How do I know an email/call isn't an AI scam?**
A: You often can't tell anymore — that's the point. So verify any money/data/access request through a *second channel* (call a known number). Don't act on urgency alone.

## The honest-answer escape hatches (use freely)
- "Great question — I want to give you the right answer, not a guess. I'll confirm and follow up."
- "That's more of a policy/IT decision — let me get you the official answer from [CONTACT]."
- "Short version: [X]. If you want the deeper why, grab me after."
