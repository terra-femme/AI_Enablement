# Lesson 4 — Using It Safely
**Time:** 7 min · **Core message:** *Verify before you send, and never paste what you couldn't email to a stranger.*

> This is the most important lesson. If you're short on time, never cut this one.

## Objective
The room internalizes two non-negotiable habits: **verify outputs** and **protect data**.

## Demo first (2 min)
Show a confident, wrong answer (a made-up statistic or a fake citation). Let the room feel the "whoa, it just made that up" moment. That fear is the lesson — channel it into the two habits.

## The 2 habits + 1 rule (rule of 3)
1. **Verify before you rely.** Check facts, numbers, names, and sources yourself. *You* are responsible for anything you submit — same as if you typed it by hand.
2. **Protect data — the stranger test.** *"If you couldn't email this to a stranger, don't paste it into AI you're not sure about."* No client data, records, SSNs, health info, or passwords into unapproved tools.
3. **Use only approved tools** ([Claude Enterprise]) — never a personal/free AI account for company work.

## The anchor analogy
> Treat AI like a **very chatty assistant in a public café.** Helpful — but assume anything sensitive you say could travel. Use the company's private room (the approved enterprise tool) for real work.

## The new-threat note (30 sec — links to your scam module)
- AI makes scams flawless: realistic phishing, **deepfake voice/video** of your boss. **Verify any money/data/access request through a second channel** (call a known number). Appearance and fluency no longer prove identity.

## Talking points / script beats
- "The old advice — 'look for typos' — is dead. New rule: **verify, don't inspect.**"
- "Report mistakes or exposure fast and without fear — we fix problems, we don't punish honesty."

## Common stumble (and the fix)
- "But the enterprise tool is private, right?" → Yes, the approved enterprise tool is the safe place *and* you should still confirm current data terms with [ADMIN]. The danger is **unapproved** consumer tools.

## So what / now what
**Now what:** two questions before every paste — "Did I verify it?" and "Would I email this to a stranger?"
