# AI 101 — Lunch & Learn (Master Outline)
### A 45-minute intro session for a non-technical team

**Goal:** in 45 minutes, take a room from "AI is magic / AI is scary" to "I know what it is, when to use it, how to stay safe, and where my work goes."
**Audience:** non-technical employees of [COMPANY NAME].
**Format:** 6 short lessons, demo-first, heavy on Q&A. Each lesson has its own file in this folder.

> Teaching rules baked into every lesson: **one idea per lesson, demo before theory, rule of 3, analogy first.**

---

## Opening (5 min) — do NOT start with slides
1. Ask the room: **"In one word, how do you feel about AI at work?"** (collect a few out loud). This lowers fear and tells you who's in the room.
2. One promise: *"By the end you won't be an expert — you'll be safe, useful, and unafraid."*
3. One ground rule: *"There are no dumb questions today. The dumb move is guessing and pasting client data."*

## Materials checklist
- [ ] Approved tool open and logged in ([Claude Enterprise]) for live demos
- [ ] A safe, **fake** example document (no real client/PHI data) for demos
- [ ] The 6 lessons (this folder) + the Q&A sheet (`../ai-101/qa_common-questions.md`)
- [ ] Leave-behind: 1-pager of "your 3 habits" (from L6) + the AUP (`../acceptable-use-policy-template.md`)
- [ ] Your speaker notes (`../presenter/presenter-best-practices.md`)

## Success = the room can answer 3 things on the way out
1. "It has a knowledge cutoff and can be confidently wrong." (L1)
2. "I verify before I send, and I never paste confidential data into unapproved tools." (L4)
3. "My work lives in Projects + the team drive." (L5)

> 📝 If you only have 20 minutes: run L1, L4, and L6. Safety + mental model + next step is the irreducible core.
